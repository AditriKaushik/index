## Definition

The high and low established in the first minutes of the session. Here the
window is **09:15–09:30** — the first 15 minutes of the NSE equity session.

## How it's used here

Two purposes, and the framework leans harder on the second:

1. A **reference range** whose boundaries become candidate breakout levels.
2. A **waiting period**. The stated instruction is to avoid rushing into opening
   noise ([[2026-08-10-1-lakh-nse-intraday-framework|framework]]), which pairs
   with the ~09:30 start of the preferred new-entry window.

## Caveats

15 minutes is a convention, not a derived number; 5-minute and 30-minute opening
ranges are equally common elsewhere and would produce different levels. Nothing
in the wiki yet justifies the choice.
