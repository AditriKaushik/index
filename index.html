Relative strength calculator

RS = +0.65% — set threshold ≈ ±0.30% while back-testing.

ATR stop idea

Back-test a stop distance around 0.75–1.0 × 5-minute ATR(14), but only when the stop also lies beyond a valid structure invalidation. Higher volatility should generally reduce quantity.

Opening-range + VWAP workflow

  1. 09:15–09:30: establish the opening range; avoid rushing into opening noise.
  2. Identify market regime: trending, ranging, or choppy.
  3. Screen only very liquid stocks showing relative strength/weakness and participation.
  4. Wait for a defined breakout/structure close rather than a one-tick breach.
  5. Prefer a successful hold/retest of the breakout level or VWAP.
  6. Before ordering: write entry, stop, quantity, rupee risk, target and estimated costs.
  7. Do not move the stop farther away merely to avoid realizing a loss.

Tax and risk notes

Do not subtract a universal tax % per trade. Intraday share transactions settled without delivery are generally treated as speculative transactions for income-tax purposes, and current ITR-3 materials separately capture intraday/speculative-business income. Annual tax depends on the complete tax situation. Keep contract notes and use a qualified tax professional.
  • No indicator or formula guarantees a profitable trade or a 1% daily return.
  • Stop orders can fill worse than the planned stop price.
  • Spread, slippage, gaps, news, liquidity and operational issues can enlarge losses.
  • Never increase risk after a loss to recover the day.
  • The framework is educational/back-testing material, not a personalized securities recommendation.
