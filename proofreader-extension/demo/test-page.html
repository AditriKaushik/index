Question 1

If the cost price of an article is ₹ 450 and the profit is 20%, find the selling price.

Solution: Profit = 20% of 450 = 80. Selling price = 450 + 80 = 530. Correct answer: (b)

Question 2

Solution: 12 + 18 + 24 + 30 = 84, and 84 ÷ 4 = 21.
