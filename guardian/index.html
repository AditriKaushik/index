How this protects you

  • End-to-end encrypted. Your location, voice and video are encrypted on your phone with a key that is part of the link itself. The server relays scrambled bytes and can never read them.
  • Nothing is stored. No account, no history, no database. When you stop, there is nothing left to leak.
  • You are always in control. Sharing starts only when you tap, it shows on screen the whole time, it stops on a timer, and one tap ends it.
  • It cannot be hidden. Guardian has no silent or invisible mode on purpose — it is for sharing with people you choose, not for watching someone without their knowledge.

One-time setup needed

Guardian needs the address of your relay server (the small Cloudflare Worker in this repo) before it can connect two phones together.

How Guardian works

In plain words:

  1. You choose what to share and tap Start sharing. Your phone asks for location, microphone or camera permission — nothing turns on without your yes.
  2. Guardian makes a one-off link. Part of that link is a secret key that never leaves your phone and is never sent to the server, because the part after the # in a web address is never transmitted.
  3. Everything you share is locked with that key before it leaves your phone (AES-256-GCM). The relay sees only scrambled bytes and the size of them.
  4. Whoever opens the link has the key, so their phone can unlock it. Voice and video skip the server entirely and travel phone-to-phone over WebRTC, itself encrypted.
  5. When the timer ends, when you tap stop, or when you close the app, the sharing ends and nothing remains anywhere.
One honest limitation: this is a web app, so if you lock your phone or switch apps for a long time, the browser may pause location updates. Keep Guardian on screen during a journey that matters, or add it to your home screen, where it keeps running longer.
Guardian is a way to stay in touch with people who care about you. It is not a way to monitor someone without their knowledge, and it is built so it cannot be used that way: the person sharing must tap start, and their phone shows it the whole time.
