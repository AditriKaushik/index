How it works

  • Turn Location, Voice or Camera on for the group. Once it is on, any member can see it whenever they want — no need to tap each time.
  • End-to-end encrypted. The key lives inside the group link, never on the server.
  • Turn any of them off, and no one in the group can access it any more.

    Good to know

    “Any time” means while their app is open. The Android app keeps running in your pocket with the screen off, so location and voice keep working. In a plain browser the tab has to stay open, and phones never allow the camera to run in the background — so camera works only while that person has Guardian on screen.
    Guardian has no hidden mode. Whatever you turn on, your own phone shows it, and you can turn it off at any moment.
