    Methodology & why not "AI"

    Full transparency on how the score above is actually computed.

    This is a deterministic, rules-based multi-factor model, not a trained machine-learning system. It encodes the framework document's own thresholds — the ±0.30% relative-strength cut, the RSI 55–70 momentum band, the regime/structure gates from its decision tree — as fixed, published weights. Every point in the score above is traceable to one of these rules; nothing is learned or hidden.

    A "neural network" label was deliberately left off. A real one needs a historical dataset of setups and outcomes to train on, and none exists here — this page has no live market feed and no trade history. Wrapping the same hand-set weights in a neural-network claim wouldn't add predictive power, only false authority, which is precisely the failure mode that turns trading into gambling.

    How to earn a real probability number

    Log every trade this page scores — direction, inputs, verdict, and the actual outcome — for at least 30–50 trades, then compute your own win rate and expectancy by score band using the back-test scorecard in the full framework. That empirical, personal hit-rate is a real probability. This score is the disciplined, auditable checklist that gets you there without curve-fitting on day one.
